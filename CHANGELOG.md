## [3.3.0] - 2026-06-12

### Added
- New course: **Analysis of Cellular Networks (ACN)**.
  - Comprehensive 11-lecture curriculum based on Uni Potsdam course materials.
  - Detailed concepts, MCQ questions (Turkish/English), and flashcards.
  - Topics: Graph Theory, Centrality, Randomization, Motifs, Clustering, GRN Inference, Flows, and Alignment.
- Registered ACN in `src/lib/courses.ts` and `src/lib/lecture-videos.ts`.
